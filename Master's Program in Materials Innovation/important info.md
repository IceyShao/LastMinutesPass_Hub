[Educational Purpose & Human Resource Development]
This program aims to cultivate individuals who will lead innovations in futuristic energy materials and the field of electronics, and who can thrive and contribute on a global stage.

The aims and overview of the Materials Innovation

Fostering Experts in Pioneering Research on Materials Science

World's Top Level Researchers and Facilities in Tsukuba Science City
Master's and Doctoral Program Conducted in English

Small Numbers but Highly Qualified Students from all over the world.

Support Students Financially through Scholarships or Research Assistant, provide a career path support.

Create Relationship and Partnership with Universities in the world.

Further Introduction of Internships through Coordination between University and Industry

If you're interested in materials science and nanoscale/micro sciences, this program is fantastic! Senpais in this program have opportunities to present in conferences, publish papers, and think this program is fun! This program also has many parties and dinners and in the past had a Christmas party consisting of members and faculty.

This program is competitive and for April or October batch the website said only 4 can be accepted. In October 2026 admissions exam, for both April and October 2027 class only 6 people are admitted. 

# Application Timing Reminder

- If applying for **April**, apply in **July the year before**
- If applying for **October**, apply in **October the year before**

1) 3-page Research Plan (including references) and Documents Submission
2) 10 minute presentation interview + 10 minutes Q-and-A
