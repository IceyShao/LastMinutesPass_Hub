[Educational Purpose & Human Resource Development]
This program aims to cultivate individuals who will lead innovations in futuristic energy materials and the field of electronics, and who can thrive and contribute on a global stage.

If you're interested in materials science and nanoscale/micro sciences, this program is fantastic! Senpais in this program have opportunities to present in conferences, publish papers, and think this program is fun! This program also has many dinners and in the past had a Christmas party consisting of members and faculty.
